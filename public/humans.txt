# EngineerOS

> AI-native workspace for notes, tasks, and projects.

## What is this?

EngineerOS is an open-source, self-hosted workspace that combines notes, tasks, projects, and daily entries into one connected system. It uses semantic search (embeddings), a knowledge graph, and an AI assistant with citations to help engineers, researchers, and builders retrieve what they know — without vendor lock-in.

## Built by

- GitHub: https://github.com/KunjShah95
- Stack: Next.js 16, Supabase (Postgres + pgvector), Tailwind CSS, React 19
- Deployed on Vercel

## Data ownership

Your data lives in your own Supabase project. No data is stored on EngineerOS servers. You can export or delete it at any time.
